Cake Club Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Cake Club is provided by William Evans. This policy explains information handled by the game and when you contact us. The current policy is available at highdrone.github.io/cake-club-support/privacy.html.

Playing on your device

You can play without creating a Cake Club account or enabling online scores. The app stores settings, progress, device high scores, and best stars for icing designs on your device. Memory, Relaxed, and two-player Bake-off records stay on the device and are not submitted to Game Center. Two-player games take turns on one device; Cake Club has no online multiplayer or player chat.

The app has no advertising, advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. It does not request your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos. It does not send local-only game records to a server operated by the publisher. Device backups may include app data according to your device and Apple account settings.

Motion controls

Cake Club reads device-motion gravity data, with an accelerometer fallback, to help you steer falling cake layers. Motion samples and calibration are used locally; raw samples are not stored or uploaded. Motion updates stop when stacking play is inactive or the app leaves the foreground. Touch controls and compatible game controllers are alternatives.

Optional Game Center scores

Online sharing is off by default. A grown-up can enable it after reading the in-app explanation. Apple handles Game Center sign-in; Cake Club does not receive your Apple account password.

When sharing is enabled, eligible completed solo Rush rounds submit the run score and highest cleared level to Apple's Game Center. These records are linked to your Game Center identity. Your Game Center profile and scores may be visible to other players through Apple's services, subject to Apple's settings and restrictions.

Cake Club uses a game-scoped player identifier to associate submissions with the correct account. If an upload fails, the app can retain that identifier and the highest pending score and level locally, then retry only when the same account is authenticated. Play without a verified account is not later assigned to another account. The game does not request your Game Center friends list.

Apple's developer tools allow the publisher to view and manage leaderboard player names and scores, including removing scores or blocking players to maintain fair leaderboards. This information supports the leaderboard feature and its management; Cake Club does not use it for advertising or tracking.

Turning off online sharing stops new submissions and clears the app's pending queue. It does not remove scores already held by Apple. Manage your profile and sharing preferences in Apple's Game Center settings. Apple describes its handling and retention of this information in Game Center & Privacy.

Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases; Cake Club does not receive payment-card details. Apple separately operates device backups, Game Center, TestFlight, and diagnostic services. Apple may provide sales, crash, diagnostic, or beta-feedback information through its developer tools according to its policies and your settings. Cake Club adds no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Apple's practices are described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support and retention

Contact wwevans@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. Emailing from the support website is optional and occurs outside the game. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer and resolve your request and retain it as needed for that purpose. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information.

You can erase local progress and scores in Kitchen settings. Local settings and records otherwise remain until you remove app data or delete the app; backups may retain copies. Pending score records are removed after successful submission or when sharing is disabled. Apple controls retention of records held by its services.

These help pages

These pages have no contact form, advertising, or analytics scripts added by Cake Club. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process connection and security information to serve and protect them. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Email support is optional and uses your chosen email service.

Families and updates

Local play requires no name, email address, or Game Center sign-in. Parents can leave online sharing off and manage children's Game Center restrictions using Apple's family and device settings. Cake Club does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

We will update this policy and its effective date if our practices change.
