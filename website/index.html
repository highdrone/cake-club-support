What you get

Unlimited levels.
Play at your pace.

Keep baking through a dynamically generated recipe sequence that remixes six bakery themes, cake flavors, frosting layouts, piping patterns, and toppings. Familiar ingredients return in different combinations. There’s no final level or fixed campaign to complete.

Always another cake.

Start with Warm-up, Creative, or Showstopper. Challenges become more demanding as you progress, then settle into a balanced difficulty range while the recipe combinations keep changing.

A quick bake or a long unwind.

Timed cakes start with roughly 3–4 minutes on the clock, depending on the recipe. Relaxed has no time limit. There’s no fixed total playtime: keep chasing scores or decorating for as long as you enjoy it.

One purchase. The whole game.

Unlimited progression, Rush, Memory, Relaxed, local two-player Bake-off, Today’s Creation, and Recipe Book practice are included. No ads, subscriptions, paid level packs, or in-app purchases.
