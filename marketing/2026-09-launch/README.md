# Cake Club launch kit — September 17, 2026

**Big cakes. Little chaos.**

Cake Club is live for iPhone and iPad. Steer falling tiers, frost and pipe directly on a 3D cake, then match the example in Rush, Memory, or Relaxed mode. One purchase includes every mode; no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Core play works offline. US price checked at launch: $0.99; regional prices vary.
