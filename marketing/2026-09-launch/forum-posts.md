Title: **I launched Cake Club, a 3D cake-building game for iPhone and iPad**

I'm William, the developer of Cake Club. It's now on the App Store, and I wanted to share the project here.

You steer falling cake tiers into place, then frost, pipe, and decorate them to match an example. Rush mode adds a timer and melting icing; Memory hides the example; Relaxed lets you take your time. It's $0.99 in the US, with no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases, and the core game works offline.

A lot of the recent work has been about making small touch interactions feel fair. You can adjust a layer while it falls, piping snaps to a guide that becomes less forgiving as difficulty rises, and frosting a side section also colors its exposed top edge. The score compares the whole cake, so rotating the camera doesn't change the result.

Disclosure: I used AI coding assistance during development and AI assistance with this launch post. The screenshots on the site show the actual game.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cake-club/id6808979979

Screenshots and gameplay details: https://playcakeclub.com

I'm William, the developer of Cake Club. I just released it for iPhone and iPad.

The cake layers drop automatically, and you can steer them in the air with tilt or touch to rescue a shaky landing. Then you frost sections, trace piping, and place toppings to match an example cake. You can rotate and zoom in to work on the details.

There are three solo modes: Rush adds time pressure and melting icing, Memory asks you to recreate a cake after studying it, and Relaxed removes the clock. There's also a local two-player Bake-off on one device.

It's $0.99 in the US; one purchase includes everything. No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Core play works offline. iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.
