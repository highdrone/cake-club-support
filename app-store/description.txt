Big cakes. Little chaos.

Build, frost, pipe, and decorate your own 3D cakes in a playful kitchen full of color and wobble. Follow the example, add your finishing touches, then turn your creation around and see how it compares.

BUILD IT YOUR WAY
Steer falling cake layers with tilt, touch, or a compatible controller. A visual compass and landing target help you line up each tier. Earn Perfect Stack and Rescue bonuses as you build, or restart a wobbly stack and try again. Just want to decorate? Switch stacking off in Settings.

DECORATE DIRECTLY ON THE CAKE
Paint icing sections marked by subtle dotted guides, smooth the surface, trace piping patterns, and place toppings where you want them. Each side section coats its exposed top edge too. Later recipes introduce narrower stripes and extra rows; Memory keeps freehand icing without section guides. Rotate the cake to reach its sides or look down from above. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in for detail or out for a wider view. After five seconds of inactivity, the view smoothly returns to its default zoom. Undo a slip, switch tools whenever you like, and tap Finish Cake when you're ready.

PIPING THAT GROWS WITH YOU
Trace inside the translucent ribbon and nearby strokes snap onto the example pattern. The allowed deviation narrows as levels rise, rewarding a steadier hand. Memory mode hides the guide for a freehand challenge.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Rush: Race the clock as the kitchen heats up and icing begins to melt. Use one Chill per cake to cool things down.
• Memory: Study the example, then recreate its colors, piping, and toppings from memory. A short Peek costs game time and points, never money.
• Relaxed: Enjoy an untimed, cool kitchen and take your time with every detail.

Two-player Bake-off lets friends take turns on the same device with the same recipe, clock, and heat.

A FRESH CAKE EVERY TIME
Dynamically generated recipes mix six themes with new combinations of flavors, frosting, piping, and toppings. Choose Warm-up, Creative, or Showstopper, try Today's Creation, or practice in the untimed Recipe Book. Collect practice stars and build your skills with no final level to reach.

COMPARE EVERY DETAIL
Inspect the example and your finished cake with easy rotate, top-view, and reset controls. Switching between Your cake and Example keeps the same viewing angle. Scoring compares the whole cake, including surfaces out of sight; moving the camera never changes your score. Separate feedback for the surface, piping, and toppings helps you decide what to try next.
