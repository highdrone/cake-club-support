Cake Club Support

Your next great cake starts here.

Cake Club is a 3D cake-decorating game for iPhone and iPad. Stack, frost, pipe, and finish your own creations in a playful bakery.

Need a hand?

Email wwevans@gmail.com for game support or privacy questions. Include your device model, iOS version, Cake Club version from Kitchen settings, and what happened. Please never send passwords or payment details.

Steer the falling layers

Tiers drop automatically after a short countdown. Tilt gently toward the steering arrow. The dot shows your input and the target ring previews the landing. Bring the ring toward the center, then level your device to brake. Early falls are slower so you can recover your aim. Missed tiers retry.

Tilt is optional. Slide anywhere in the bakery or use the steering compass by touch. Pause > Simple controls lets you turn tilt off; resuming recalibrates your comfortable holding angle. Tap the double arrow to fall faster when lined up.

Paint, smooth, and decorate

Once the tiers land, tap a physical tool to see its choices. Choose icing, the smoother, a piping color, or a topping, then touch the cake. Use two fingers to turn the cake and raise the camera, or tap the viewfinder for an overhead view. Extra toppings stay on your creation. Pause includes Undo.

Follow the piping ribbon

Choose the example’s piping color, then trace inside the translucent ribbon. Nearby strokes snap onto the pattern. The guide gradually narrows at higher levels. Wrong colors and strokes outside the ribbon stay freehand. Lift to switch between nearby lines. Undo removes a whole stroke. Memory mode hides the guide and keeps piping unassisted.

Rush, Memory, and Relaxed

Rush challenges you against the clock. Heat makes painted frosting soften and drip. One Chill per cake provides 20 seconds of cooling and up to six extra seconds, capped at the original time allowance.

Memory gives you 12 seconds to study the order. After you begin, its picture and matching guides disappear. Peek reveals it for three seconds at a cost of eight seconds and 25 score points. These are game costs only; there are no purchases.

Relaxed has no timer or heat. Two-player Bake-off takes turns on one device. Today’s Creation offers an untimed daily recipe, and the Recipe Book lets you practice. Best stars and mastery are saved locally.

Serve and try again

Ring the bell when every tier has landed. You can serve without a perfect match or complete frosting coverage. Your actual cake stays on screen for judging. Retry or continue to another cake from the results.

Sound and controllers

Kitchen settings includes separate music and sound-effect controls, plus optional meme moments, haptics, and celebrations. Audio respects your device’s Silent mode. Compatible controllers steer with the left stick; A makes a tier fall faster, Menu pauses, and Y serves when ready. Use touch or a mouse to choose tools and decorate.

Optional online scores

Every recipe can be played offline. Game Center is off by default. Kitchen settings or High scores lets a grown-up review the sharing explanation before enabling it. Only eligible solo Rush scores and levels are shared. Other modes remain on your device. Turning sharing off clears pending submissions but does not delete scores already held by Apple.

Cake Club has no ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or player chat. Read our privacy policy for details about local records, Game Center, motion controls, and support.

Earlier versions

These instructions describe version 1.0, build 14 and the build 13 gameplay it preserves. Older versions used different stacking and camera controls. Update the app to use automatic drops and guided piping.
